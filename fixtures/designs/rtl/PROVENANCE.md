All files are in-house fixtures, copied byte for byte; sha256 values are
recorded in each design manifest (`fixtures/designs/<name>.json`).

|---|---|---|---|

| `dense/dense.sv` | RTL written by RustROAD `scripts/verify_power_io.py` (tap-release run `.work/tap-release-final/sky130.sv`, identical to `gf180.sv`) | RustROAD tap stream (`4a42dfc`) | Apache-2.0 |

fixture and is not copied.
