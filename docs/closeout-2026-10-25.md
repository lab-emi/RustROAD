## Changes and acceptance

- GF180 DRV closure now co-sizes drivers/buffers, protects hold paths and has
  bounded additional candidate/layout work. The 1.0 ns maximum-transition
  limit and the frozen pre-layout clock-period rule remain unchanged.
- The [MPW declaration policy](mpw-deviations.md) keeps the raw original error,
  brackets a reference driver-waveform step and requires raw agreement within
  0.2 ps at a nearby sample across it, plus an independent transistor/RC vote.
  It never relaxes endpoint parity. Failed and incomplete proofs remain failures.
- The mult declaration experiment was rejected by that policy. Its physical
  clock-leaf fanout was reduced from eight to four, as was regfile/SKY130's,
  reducing loading and restoring ordinary timing/annotation acceptance. Their
  SDCs and periods are unchanged; no special timing waiver is used for them.
- Hold-cell removal is not always a fault after other cells were resized. A
  removal that both engines prove still meets hold is recorded, and a separate
  explicit 2 ns hold-uncertainty fault must be rejected by both. Real misses and
  incomplete comparisons continue to fail the control gate.
- Installed tool locations, current Verilator's public JSON interface, GNU time
  discovery and inherited memory limits are handled explicitly. Original tool
  identities are retained; current results were regenerated with the qualified
  tools. SERV provenance now distinguishes its Apache-2.0 decoder from ISC files.

The largest ordinary endpoint difference was 0.786409 ps against the 1 ps
limit. Maximum observed per-process peak RSS was 520.2 MiB. The three
parallel runners were confined to an 8 GiB RAM / 2 GiB swap cgroup with a finite
wall-clock guard. Timings include complete flows, extraction, tool startup,
negative controls and the deterministic rebuild; they are shared-host results.

Validation also includes 137 local Python tests and the corresponding CI,
plus the root integration and RustSTA scope/safety checks documented in the
RustEDA superproject. The implementation identity and original branch are retained in the raw receipts.

## Limits

This is block-level qualification, not foundry tapeout signoff. Density is
reported separately; IR/EM, SI, OCV/CPPR, fill, pad/seal structures and industrial
scale are not newly verified here. OpenC910 checker reproduction still lacks
its original raw case; full GF180 latch timing and high-R Ceff remain explicit
limits. Independent legal, foundry and engineering reviews are not self-certified.
The superproject publishes the current [scope and limitations](https://lab-emi.github.io/RustEDA/verification/). Original review and decision records are preserved locally.
